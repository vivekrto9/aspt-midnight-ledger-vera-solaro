# Design Philosophy — Vera Solaro Midnight Ledger

> Scope: every public, booking, account, auth, error, and closed-state surface.

## Identity

Vera's Midnight Ledger is an archival night register: deep ink, pale paper, violet observations, mint status marks, monospaced annotations, hand-built geometry, and quiet editorial copy. It is specific and restrained, not a generic dark theme.

## Principles

1. **The source establishes the composition.** Preserve its hierarchy, type roles, palette, rules, texture, celestial forms, and strong light/dark section rhythm.
2. **Production truth sits behind the pixels.** Prototype values never replace live booking, payment, account, delivery, or provider state.
3. **One offer means one visible offer.** Only the current Natal Hour appears in public registers and links; hidden capabilities remain backend-compatible.
4. **The ledger is the component system.** Use sections, registers, dividers, and typographic columns before introducing cards.
5. **Missing media stays honest.** Use registered assets only in truthful roles and retain intentional placeholders everywhere else.
6. **Reflow without erasing identity.** Mobile keeps the booking path, ledger rhythm, celestial anchor, readable long-form copy, and accessible state changes.
7. **Editing remains complete.** Public static copy and SEO stay inside the existing Content Studio contract.

## Avoid

- Warm almanac colours, generic wellness gradients, dashboard mosaics, glass effects, or unrelated component-library styling.
- Remote fonts, raw Calendly embeds, browser-owned payment truth, fake availability, or invented confirmations.
- Reintroducing the monthly-letter offer, unavailable readings, prototype screen directories, or warm-home-only sections.
- Hard-coded visitor copy outside the canonical content registry or duplicated asset/content contracts.
- Motion without hierarchy, hidden keyboard focus, or mobile overflow.

## Adaptation boundary

Project owners may edit public copy through Content Studio and replace customer media through Project Assets. A theme extension must use the Ledger tokens coherently and must not change the template's deployment, privacy, or authoritative business-state contracts.
